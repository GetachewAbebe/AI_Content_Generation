# Story brief: የጀበናው መጀመሪያ ጭስ (The First Smoke of the Jebena)

Supplied by the author on 25 November 2026. This is the author's script; picture prompts in `storyboard.json` were expanded from it.

- **Logline:** Before the city wakes, an Ethiopian grandmother's dawn coffee ritual becomes a silent prayer and living thread connecting her family across distance and time.
- **Language:** Amharic narration, burned-in English subtitles. **Target:** 75 s.
- **Audience:** Ethiopian young adults and parents, in Addis Ababa and in the diaspora, watching on mobile phones (9:16) and desktops (16:9).
- **Feeling:** a quiet ache of gratitude and warmth; the love of home isn't loud, it lives in small repeated rituals done before anyone else is awake.
- **Consent:** fictionalised composite character ("Emama Almaz"), synthetic voice and visuals; no real person's likeness or cloned voice.
- **Music:** warm, intimate Ethiopian acoustic instrumental (masenqo, krar, subtle washint), no vocals, 72 BPM, slow, nostalgic, tender, uplifting toward shots 07-09. Ducked under narration, building in the 1.5 s pauses between shots.
